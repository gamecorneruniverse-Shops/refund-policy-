01

Overview & Scope

This Refund and Return Policy applies to all purchases completed through GCU-SHOPS (gcushops.kesug.com), managed by Game Corner Universe Limited.

Our objective is to ensure transparency and confidence when purchasing electronics, gaming accessories, and gift parcels across Bangladesh. These terms operate under the Consumer Rights Protection Act of Bangladesh.

02

Return Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for an exchange, return, or refund, your purchase must satisfy the following baseline standards:

  • You must raise a claim within 72 hours (3 calendar days) from the delivery confirmation timestamp.
  • The item must be unused, unaltered, and in the exact condition you received it.
  • All original packaging, warranty documentation, manuals, tags, barcodes, and complimentary gifts must be completely intact.
  • A valid order number, digital invoice, or payment transaction reference must be provided.
03

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Goods

If you receive a package that shows physical transit trauma, manufacturing defects, or wrong models/colors, GCU-SHOPS will replace the item free of extra cost or provide a full refund if replacement stock is depleted.

Any visible physical damage or missing component must be reported immediately upon parcel receipt to our WhatsApp support team.

04

Unboxing Video Verification

To prevent fraudulent claims and expedite resolution with courier partners, customers are strongly encouraged to record a continuous unboxing video showing the intact outer courier bag, seal opening, and item condition.

Important: Claims regarding physically cracked surfaces, missing bundle accessories, or empty parcel issues may require unboxing video verification to be validated.
05

Non-Returnable & Non-Refundable Items

Unless verified to have arrived factory-damaged or completely inoperative, the following categories cannot be returned:

  • Digital game codes, voucher gift codes, software keys, and mobile top-ups once delivered or revealed.
  • Personal hygiene wearables such as open in-ear earphones/earbuds where the factory seal has been broken.
  • Products damaged by power surges, water contact, unauthorized repair attempts, or improper usage.
  • Clearance items explicitly marked as "Final Sale" or "As-Is".
06

Misty Box & Mystery Box Offers

By purchasing a Misty Box or Mystery Box offer, you acknowledge that its exact contents are undisclosed by design as detailed in Section 15 of our General Terms.

Mystery Boxes are strictly non-refundable based on personal taste or dissatisfaction with randomized content. Returns for Mystery Boxes are only accepted if an internal electronic device arrives dead-on-arrival (DOA) or damaged during shipping.

07

Step-by-Step Claim Procedure

Follow these quick steps to open a return or replacement ticket:

  • Step 1: Contact GCU Support via WhatsApp (01913196903) or email (gamecorneruniverse@gmail.com).
  • Step 2: State your Order ID and attach photographs or a short video demonstrating the issue.
  • Step 3: Our team will review the claim within 24 to 48 hours and provide shipping instructions or arrange courier return pickup.
  • Step 4: Safely pack the product with all its accessories and ship it to our designated inspection depot.
08

Return Inspection & Approval

Once your returned parcel reaches our verification facility, our technical team will inspect the item within 2 business days to confirm the reported fault.

If approved, we will initiate an identical product replacement or issue your refund according to your selected settlement method.

09

Refund Methods & Payout Timelines

Approved refunds are processed in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT ৳) using the same channel originally used for purchase:

  • MFS (bKash / Nagad / Rocket): 3 to 7 business days following inspection clearance.
  • Bank Transfer / Cards: 5 to 10 business days depending on issuer clearance cycles.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Disbursed via customer's chosen bKash or Nagad personal wallet.
10

Return Shipping Costs

If the return is due to our error (defective product, missing item, incorrect shipment), GCU-SHOPS will reimburse the return courier fee.

If an exchange is granted due to change of mind (where mutually agreed upon in writing), the customer is responsible for both return delivery and subsequent shipping fees.

11

Order Cancellations

Orders may be canceled free of charge before they are handed over to our logistical courier partner. Once an order is picked up and assigned an active tracking number, it cannot be canceled mid-transit and must follow standard return processing.

12

Disputes & Official Contact Desk

If you have any questions or disputes regarding a pending return or refund, please reach out to our dedicated support representatives directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days do I have to request a refund?

You must reach out within 72 hours (3 calendar days) after receiving the parcel to file an eligible claim.

What if I threw away the original box?

Unfortunately, products must be returned in their original packaging with manuals and tags intact. Without original packaging, refunds or replacements cannot be authorized.

How will I get my refund for Cash on Delivery (COD) purchases?

For COD orders, our support desk will contact you to send your payout directly to your verified bKash or Nagad personal wallet once the product passes physical inspection.

Can I return a mystery or misty box if I don't like what I got?

No. Mystery boxes are sold based on randomized contents. Returns are solely permitted if an included item arrives broken or mechanically defective.
